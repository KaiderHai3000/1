Tax questions

Moving to Switzerland also changes your tax situation — especially in the year of the move, it's worth taking a close look to avoid double taxation.

Withholding tax and the double taxation agreement

Many new arrivals are initially taxed at source in Switzerland. The double taxation agreement between Germany and Switzerland governs which country has the right to tax in which cases, so income isn't taxed twice.

Tax residency

Where you're tax resident depends on your home and the centre of your life — this isn't always clear-cut, especially in the transition year. We help place your individual situation and refer you to specialised tax advice where needed.
